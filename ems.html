PCB Manufacturing

High-quality bare PCBs with fast lead times and multiple options.

  • Substrate: FR4, metal, polyimide, etc.
  • Layer count: 1-20 layers
  • Min. line/space: 3/3 mil (75/75 µm)

SMT Assembly

Precision SMT assembly for a wide range of components.

  • Component size: 0201 metric and above
  • BGA pitch: 0.35mm and above
  • Placement accuracy: ±25 μm (3σ)
  • Dimensions: 50×50mm ~ 460×510mm

THT Assembly

Reliable through-hole assembly with wave or selective soldering.

  • Hole size: 0.6mm and above
  • Component lead: Axial, radial, connectors
  • Wave solder: Dual wave with N2 protection
  • Hand soldering: For odd-form, heat-sensitive

Supply Models

Turnkey, consignment, or partial

The supply model determines who sources your components and how pricing is structured. Understanding the difference helps you choose the right approach for your project.

Turnkey Manufacturing

You provide files. We source everything, build everything, and deliver finished boards.

Pricing: Assembly + Components + Sourcing Fee

Best For: Hardware startups and founders without existing supply chain relationships. Products where Indian market sourcing delivers the best combination of price and availability.

You receive a line-by-line BOM cost breakdown before we order anything. No hidden margins.

Partial Consignment

You supply specific components. We source everything else and manage the complete build.

Pricing: Assembly + Partial Sourcing Fee

Best For: Established brands with existing supplier relationships for key components (proprietary parts, specific qualified components).

We source all standard passive components, connectors, and ICs from India and globally. Your supplied components are not marked up.

Full Consignment

You supply all components. We provide assembly, test, and inspection only.

Pricing: Assembly Only

Best For: Established OEMs with mature supply chains and strict AVL requirements where all components must come from specific qualified suppliers.

Ship kitted components to our Hyderabad facility — we assemble, test, and ship finished boards back to your distribution center.

Quality & Compliance

Built to IPC standards with full production traceability

Every build runs through a defined quality system — inspection at every stage, documented results per unit, and compliance with the standards your product needs to reach market.

IPC-A-610 Class 2

Globally recognized standard for the acceptability of electronic assemblies. Defines acceptance criteria for solder joints, component placement, cleanliness, and cosmetic condition.

IPC J-STD-001

Defines requirements for soldering electrical and electronic assemblies. Processes are qualified to Class 2, with Class 3 available for high-reliability builds.

IPC-6012 Class 2

Covers qualification and performance specification for rigid PCBs. We verify incoming quality before any boards enter assembly — incoming inspection is not optional.

IPC-7711/7721

Rework and repair is performed to standard. All rework is documented with reason, method, and technician responsible. Reworked units are retested and documented.
