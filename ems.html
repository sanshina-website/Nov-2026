Supply Models

Turnkey, consignment, or partial

The supply model determines who sources your components and how pricing is structured.

Turnkey Manufacturing

You provide files. We source everything in India and globally, build everything, and deliver finished boards.

Pricing: Assembly + Components + Sourcing Fee

Partial Consignment

You supply specific custom ICs. We source the standard passives and manage the build locally.

Pricing: Assembly + Partial Sourcing Fee

Full Consignment

You supply all components. We provide state-of-the-art assembly, testing, and inspection.

Pricing: Assembly Only
